01 / Our story

Rooted in
the good
things.

Wine is more than what’s in the glass. It’s the land, the hands, the patience, and the people who make every pour mean something.

Vinea brings the world of wine to life through thoughtfully curated experiences, passionate people and a shared appreciation for the craft. We celebrate the makers, the stories and the moments that deserve to be savoured.
